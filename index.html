02 Services

Architectural and planning data is abundant — yet unlike most other data, it is graphic, layered, and meaningful only when interpreted by trained professionals. Drawings, regulations, and spatial documents resist automation precisely because they demand reading, not just processing.

Sadeh Studio bridges this gap, combining technological capability with high-level professional knowledge to decipher complex spatial situations and turn them into structured, accessible information. We offer consulting services across two complementary tracks — professional architectural expertise, and software and AI systems development.

Track 01

Architecture & Planning

  • Urban planning knowledge and statutory interpretation
  • Architectural assessment and professional consultation
  • Domain expertise for AI training and model validation
  • Academic research and feasibility analysis
Track 02

Software Engineering

  • Software architecture and system design
  • Database design and management
  • AI integration and development
  • Data processing and analysis
